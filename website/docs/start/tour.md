## Follow the adventure at your own pace

| Time | What PhValheim does | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Brings worlds, mods and your party's setup together. | [Explore the guides](../index.md) |
| 0:05 | Runs in Docker; a wizard helps you configure the server. | [Install](install.md) |
| 0:10 | Manages multiple vanilla and modded worlds from your browser. | [Create your first world](first-world.md) |
| 0:15 | Lets you select mods from three catalogues and pin versions. | [Choose your mods](../host/mods.md) |
| 0:20 | Edits mod configurations and preserves saved overrides through updates. | [Tune the settings](../host/mods.md#change-configurations) |
| 0:25 | Resolves dependencies and packages mods and shared configuration for players. Server-only configs stay private. | [Build the shared setup](../host/mods.md) |
| 0:30 | Syncs changed files through the PhValheim client before launching a modded game. | [Join a world](../play/join.md) |
| 0:35 | Provides backups, restores, player access controls and logs. | [Protect your world](../host/backups.md) |
| 0:40 | Gives Hugin the tools to investigate problems and propose changes for your review. | [Ask Hugin](../host/hugin.md) |
| 0:45 | Helps write, build and package a mod with Hugin's Vibes and its optional Build Pak. | [Make your first Vibe](../vibes/first-mod.md) |
| 0:50 | Publishes your mod to your own local catalogue so you can select it on a test world. | [Package and publish](../vibes/publishing.md) |
| 0:55 | Gets you ready for the next adventure. | [Start hosting](install.md) |

For a mod-list change, save the selection and update the world, then have players sync before joining. For shared config changes, **Save & apply to players** rebuilds the config payload; players receive it on their next launch. Vanilla worlds use ordinary Valheim without the PhValheim client. AI features are optional.
