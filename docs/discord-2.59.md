# 🐦 PhValheim 2.59 — let Hugin cook

**Your next Valheim mod can start with one sentence.**

> "Give me a small, configurable stamina tweak."

Meet **Hugin's Vibes**, your mod workshop inside PhValheim. Open **Tools → Hugin's Vibes**, create a Vibe, and tell the raven what you want. Hugin writes the C#, builds it, reads the compiler's complaints, and works through the fixes with you.

**He does the fiddly setup, too.** The optional Build Pak brings in the compiler, .NET, Git, BepInEx and Harmony. Hugin gathers the Valheim libraries from your world's installed server files and prepares them for modding. The game libraries stay on your server.

Click **Package** and he assembles the plugin, manifest, README and icon into a catalogue-compatible ZIP. Every Vibe has its own **local Git repository**, history, conversation and project notes.

**Your mod. Your local catalogue. Your world.**

**Publish to Catalogue** puts it in your own **Hugin's Vibes** catalogue, beside Thunderstore and Hexium. Select it in a world's Mods list, update the world, sync your client, and play. Ask for another tweak and repeat.

After the one-time setup, you can **start vibe coding in seconds** and try the result on a test world through the normal mod workflow. Use a compatible hosted or self-hosted coding model.

Already have a project? **Import from GitHub or GitLab**, or push your Vibes there. Download ZIPs for Thunderstore or Hexium when you're ready to share more widely.

Live tool activity, build feedback, context usage, automatic compaction, a mobile-friendly workbench… and a raven who gets a little excited while coding.

**Bring an idea. Let Hugin handle the workshop.**
